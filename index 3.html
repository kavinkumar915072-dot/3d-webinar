Got Questions?

Got Questions? I've Got Answers.

I have almost zero followers on LinkedIn. Can you still help me?
Yes. Many of my clients started with very few followers. The strategy focuses on building credibility from the ground up, not on follower count.
Do I need to create the content myself?
No. You can share your ideas and experiences, and I'll help turn them into posts and scripts that sound like you.
How much time do I need to invest every week?
Most clients need about 30 to 60 minutes a week for reviews and approvals, depending on the plan you choose.
How long does it take to see LinkedIn growth?
Visible changes in engagement often appear within a few weeks of consistent posting. Meaningful growth in inbound opportunities usually builds over several months.
Do you manage my LinkedIn account completely?
I can handle as much or as little as you need, from strategy and scripting only to full profile and content management. You always approve what goes live.
Can you work with people from different industries?
Yes. The principles of a strong personal brand apply across industries, and I tailor the approach to your field and audience.
What happens during the 1:1 LinkedIn audit session?
We spend 60 minutes on a video call reviewing your profile, goals, and content gaps. You receive a detailed dossier and a personalised action plan afterwards.
How do I get started?
Fill in the form above or book your 1:1 audit session. We'll confirm the details and schedule a time that works for you.
